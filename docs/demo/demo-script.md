# Demo script (about 5 minutes)

1. **Scan the QR.** Tablet shows the worker app for Compressor #01 with its last problem.
2. **Ask by voice.** Hold the mic and say, "Compressor is overheating." Show the text, confirm it.
3. **Safety screen.** If the job is dangerous, a red checklist appears and steps stay locked until every box is ticked.
4. **Steps with source.** Short steps, a yes/no question each, the manual name and page, and the highlighted page.
5. **Not found.** Ask something outside the manual. The app says it can't find it and shows "Escalate to lead engineer".
6. **Upload the SOP live.** In the admin app, upload the 5-10 page SOP, then ask a question from it and show the answer.
7. **Admin dashboard.** Common faults, unresolved faults, and questions the manuals couldn't answer.
